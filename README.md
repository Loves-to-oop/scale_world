# Ten million times bigger

Walk or jetpack through the microscopic world at ×10,000,000: a nanometre
becomes a centimetre, an atom a grain of sand, DNA 2 cm wide, a bacterium a
20 m truck, an animal cell a 200 m building, a hair an 800 m ridge.

Open `scale_world.html` (double-click works). Every size is a measured value
×1e7 (one constant in `scene.js`); every motion -- water diffusion, flagella at
100 Hz, E. coli run-and-tumble at 25 µm/s -- uses its real rate at a chosen
time scale (`[` `]`: 1 picosecond to 1 second per second).

Rebuild after editing `scene.js`: `python3 build.py`.
Values: BioNumbers; Milo & Phillips, *Cell Biology by the Numbers* (2015).
